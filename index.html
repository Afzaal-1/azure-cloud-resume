About

I came into IT through an associate degree in IT support, then a B.S. in Information Technology while working through certifications on my own time. Most of what I know about Azure I learned by building things, watching them fail, and working out why.

That's not a figure of speech. On one build, an App Service deployment kept failing with nothing useful in the error output. I went through PowerShell debug logs until I found it was hitting a regional tier quota — then fixed it and wrote it down. The failures are in the repositories alongside the working configurations, because that's the part that actually taught me something.

Alongside the lab work, I've run real client infrastructure: provisioning Azure VMs for a live workload, setting up snapshots and a recovery VM, hosting a site on App Service, and handling domains, DNS and business email — while being the only technical person my client had to call.
